## Priority 1: China ($50B+ Market)

### Market Size
- China's gaming industry: $23.4B revenue in H1 2025 (14% YoY growth)
- Overall market projected to exceed $50B in 2025 (Niko Partners)
- WeChat Mini Games alone: $3.2B revenue in H1 2025 (40% YoY growth)
- WeChat MAU: 1.4B+, 84.4% of gamers in China have played a mini game

### WeChat Mini Games (Primary Channel)
- **What:** HTML5-based games running inside WeChat app
- **Technology:** Web-based — your Three.js game is technically compatible
- **Monetization:** IAP (68.7% of revenue) and IAA/ads (31.3%)
- **Scale:** Trending games can receive up to 400K yuan (~$55K) in UA budget from platform
- **Gender split:** 17.7% of women play mini games daily vs 10.3% of men — broader than traditional gaming

### Requirements & Challenges
- **Publishing License (ISBN):** Required from National Press and Publication Administration. Foreign developers need a Chinese partner.
- **Local Entity:** Must either establish a Chinese company or partner with a licensed publisher
- **Content Review:** Game content must pass government review (no gambling mechanics, limited violence)
- **Payment:** Must integrate WeChat Pay / Alipay
- **Recommendation:** Partner with a Chinese publisher who handles licensing, payment, and distribution. They typically take 30-50% but remove all regulatory burden.

### TapTap (Secondary Channel)
- **What:** Chinese game discovery and distribution platform (like Steam for mobile)
- **Audience:** More hardcore/enthusiast gamers
- **Revenue model:** No cut from sales (ad-supported platform discovery)
- **Good for:** Community building, wishlisting, reviews

### China Strategy
1. Partner with a Chinese mini-game publisher
2. Adapt game for portrait mode (WeChat standard)
3. Simplify for shorter session lengths (WeChat users play in bursts)
4. Monetize via IAP (character skins, power-ups) + rewarded ads
5. Budget: $5-15K for publisher onboarding and localization

## Priority 2: Brazil ($2.3B Market)

### Market Overview
- Revenue: $2.3B (2025), 5.1% YoY growth
- 12th largest gaming market globally
- Largest in Latin America
- ~85M gamers

### Opportunity
- Portuguese (Brazilian) localization reaches ~230M speakers across Brazil and Portugal
- Mobile-first market — browser games are highly accessible
- Growing middle class with increasing disposable income for entertainment
- Underserved by western indie developers who skip Brazilian Portuguese localization

### Localization Cost
- Brazilian Portuguese translation: ~$0.08-0.12/word
- For a 15K word game: ~$1,200-1,800
- UI adaptation: Minimal (Latin script, similar UI patterns)

### Distribution
- Android dominant (cheap smartphones)
- Browser gaming very popular (data-conscious users prefer not to download)
- Steam Brazil has localized pricing (games often 40-60% cheaper than US)

### Influencers
- Brazilian gaming YouTube is massive (several channels 10M+ subs)
- Portuguese-language gaming content has huge audiences with less competition for indie features

## Priority 3: Turkey ($1.2B Market)

### Market Overview
- Revenue: $1.2B (2025)
- **Fastest growing:** 28% increase in consumer spending in 2024
- Young population: Median age 31, high gaming engagement
- ~40M gamers

### Opportunity
- Turkish localization is rare for indie games — huge differentiation
- Very price-sensitive market; $5 or less is the sweet spot
- Mobile gaming dominant
- Browser-first approach perfectly suited (avoiding download friction on limited data plans)

### Localization Cost
- Turkish translation: ~$0.08-0.10/word
- For 15K word game: ~$1,200-1,500
- Localization demand from Turkey grew 6x between 2015-2022

### Distribution
- Mobile/browser primary
- Steam Turkey has deeply discounted regional pricing
- YouTube and Twitch gaming communities are active

## Priority 4: Indonesia ($1.9B Market)

### Market Overview
- Revenue: $1.9B (2025), largest in Southeast Asia
- 148M active gamers (out of 275M population)
- Rapidly growing middle class
- Mobile-first market (smartphone penetration high, PC ownership lower)

### Opportunity
- Indonesian localization is extremely rare in indie games
- 88% of Indonesian consumers choose products in their language when given a choice
- Localization demand grew 4x from 2015-2022
- Young population with high gaming engagement
- Browser games are ideal: No download required on data-limited plans

### Localization Cost
- Indonesian translation: ~$0.06-0.08/word (lower cost due to translator availability)
- For 15K word game: ~$900-1,200
- Cost-effective to implement with high potential return

## Language-Specific Notes

### What to Localize
For a twin-stick shooter with minimal text, localization is relatively simple:
- Menu/UI text (~500-1,000 words)
- Tutorial text (~1,000-2,000 words)
- Weapon/enemy descriptions (~2,000-3,000 words)
- Achievement names/descriptions (~500 words)
- Store page / marketing materials (~2,000 words)
- **Total per language:** ~6,000-8,000 words (lower than typical narrative games)

### Localization at this scale is achievable for <$1,000 per language
- Real cost estimate: $500-800 per language for non-CJK
- Chinese/Japanese: $1,500-2,500 due to complexity
- Total for 6 priority languages: ~$5,000-8,000
