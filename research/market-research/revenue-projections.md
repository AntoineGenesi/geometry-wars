# Revenue Projections

## Methodology
Projections based on:
1. Comparable title performance (Geometry Wars franchise, Vampire Survivors, Brotato, etc.)
2. Steam review-to-sales multiplier (30-50x reviews = estimated units sold)
3. Browser game platform revenue benchmarks (Poki, CrazyGames)
4. Industry median data for indie games (GameDiscoverCo, VG Insights)

## Pricing Strategy Analysis

### Why $4.99 (Not $9.99 or $14.99)

| Price Point | Volume Multiplier | Revenue/Unit | Expected Net Result |
|-------------|-------------------|-------------|-------------------|
| $2.99 | 5x | $2.09 | Lower total (too cheap for perceived value) |
| **$4.99** | **3x** | **$3.49** | **Optimal sweet spot** |
| $9.99 | 1.5x | $6.99 | Comparable total, smaller audience |
| $14.99 | 1x | $10.49 | Highest per-unit, smallest audience |

**Evidence:** Every breakout indie in this genre since 2020 has been priced $3-5. Vampire Survivors ($4.99), Brotato ($4.99), 20 Minutes Till Dawn ($2.99). The $15-20 tier (Nex Machina, traditional twin-stick shooters) consistently underperforms on volume.

### Recommended Pricing
- **Browser:** Free (drives traffic, builds community, feeds Steam wishlists)
- **Steam:** $4.99 (proven optimal price for genre)
- **Mobile:** Free + $2.99 ad removal + cosmetic IAP
- **Console (if ported):** $9.99-14.99 (console audiences accept higher prices)

## Break-Even Analysis

### Costs to Reach Market (Scenario 2)

| Expense | Cost |
|---------|------|
| Steam publishing fee | $100 |
| Apple Developer Program | $99/yr |
| Marketing budget | $5,000-10,000 |
| Localization (3 languages) | $2,000-3,000 |
| Audio/music licensing or creation | $0 (already built) |
| Domain + hosting | $200/yr |
| **Total Investment** | **$7,400-13,400** |

**Break-even at $4.99 Steam price (70% rev share):**
- Need ~2,100-3,800 Steam sales to break even
- At 15% wishlist conversion: Need ~14,000-25,000 wishlists
- Very achievable with Steam Next Fest + basic marketing

### Time Investment
- Development time already invested: significant (160+ files, 1,250+ tests)
- Remaining for market-ready: 2-4 months polishing, Electron wrapper, store page
- Marketing time: 4-8 months pre-launch community building (part-time)
- **Opportunity cost is the main expense**, not cash outlay

## Key Revenue Insights

1. **Browser-first is a unique advantage.** No competitor offers "play instantly in browser, then buy on Steam for offline/enhanced version." This is a genuine differentiator.

2. **The free browser version IS the marketing.** Every browser play is a potential Steam customer. Poki's 100M monthly players are your top-of-funnel.

3. **Price low, volume high.** The data overwhelmingly supports $4.99 for this genre. Every $1 increase above $5 loses more customers than it gains in margin.

4. **International localization has outsized ROI.** $5,000 in localization could unlock $50B+ in addressable market across underserved regions.

5. **Console is gravy, not the meal.** Don't port until browser+Steam proves the game works. Console porting should be funded by proven revenue, not hope.

6. **Long-tail matters.** Twin-stick shooters sell for years. Vampire Survivors is still in Steam top sellers 3+ years after launch. Plan for a 3-5 year revenue tail, not just launch week.
