# Market 14: Italy

**Researched:** 2026-02-10
**Market Size:** Major European gaming market. Growing indie development scene. Strong retro gaming appreciation.
**Primary Platforms:** YouTube, Twitch, Discord, Instagram
**Language:** Italian

---

## Market Overview

Italy has a vibrant gaming YouTube scene with creators blending humor, nostalgia, and gameplay. The Italian gaming community has strong retro gaming appreciation, making a Geometry Wars-style arcade game culturally appealing. Milan Games Week is the primary gaming event. The indie dev scene is smaller than Germany/France/UK but growing, with studios like MixedBag, Storm in a Teacup, and others producing quality titles. Italian gamers are active on YouTube and Twitch, with several creators having multi-million subscriber counts.

## Key Takeaways for Italy Market

1. **Andrew Channel, Gabby 16 Bit, and Vintage People** are the highest-relevance creators -- they specifically cover strategy, retro, and indie games.
2. **Lucca Comics & Games is massive** (300K+ visitors) and includes gaming sections -- cultural event with high crossover appeal.
3. **Milan Games Week** is Italy's primary gaming event for consumer exposure.
4. **First Playable in Florence** is the B2B event for developer networking and game pitching.
5. **Multiplayer.it** is the essential Italian gaming media outlet.
6. **Italian retro gaming appreciation** makes a Geometry Wars-style arcade game culturally resonant.
