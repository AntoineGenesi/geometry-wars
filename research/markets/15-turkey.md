# Market 15: Turkey

**Researched:** 2026-02-10
**Market Size:** Rapidly growing gaming market. Istanbul is the world's mobile gaming capital. Strong indie dev scene producing global hits (Liar's Bar, BattleBit).
**Primary Platforms:** YouTube, Twitch (partially blocked, migrating to Kick), Discord, Instagram
**Language:** Turkish

---

## Market Overview

Turkey's gaming market is booming. Istanbul holds the title of world's mobile gaming capital, with Turkish developers having 8 titles in the top-grossing 100 on Apple's App Store. PC indie developers have shipped global hits like Liar's Bar and BattleBit. The streaming landscape is in flux -- many Turkish streamers have migrated from Twitch to Kick after the Turkish government began partially blocking Twitch in early 2024. Gaming content is a cultural phenomenon in Turkey, with creators incorporating local humor and cultural references.

## Key Takeaways for Turkey Market

1. **Turkey's indie dev scene is producing global hits** (Liar's Bar, BattleBit) -- the audience understands and supports indie games.
2. **Istanbul = world's mobile gaming capital** -- browser-based game accessible on mobile devices has strong appeal.
3. **Twitch-to-Kick migration** creates opportunity on emerging platforms.
4. **Shimo is the best creator target** -- covers mobile and indie games with relaxed commentary style.
5. **Gaming Istanbul (GIST)** is the primary event for market entry.
6. **Price-sensitive market** -- free browser-based game has inherent appeal.
