# Market 8: France

**Researched:** 2026-02-10
**Market Size:** Major European gaming market. Strong indie development culture (Dead Cells, Hades-inspiring French studios). 4th largest gaming market in Europe.
**Primary Platforms:** Twitch, YouTube, Discord, Twitter/X
**Language:** French

---

## Market Overview

France has a strong appreciation for indie games and a vibrant development scene (Motion Twin/Dead Cells, Shiro Games/Northgard, many more). French Twitch viewership is among the highest in Europe, with multiple streamers in the global top 50. Paris Games Week is the second most-visited gaming trade show in Europe (317K visitors). The ZEVENT charity streaming marathon is a uniquely French cultural phenomenon that brings together top French streamers. France's "Made in France" games initiative highlights domestic indie game development.

## Key Takeaways for France Market

1. **Indie Game Nation is the perfect event** -- 100% indie focused, B2B meetings, networking sessions. 8th edition shows it's well-established.
2. **ZeratoR and ZEVENT community** provide authentic indie game exposure -- ZeratoR personally supports indie devs and organizes France's biggest streaming event.
3. **MisterMV is a high-priority outreach target** -- retro and indie game specialist who streams roguelites and action games. Perfect genre fit.
4. **France has a strong "Made in France" game culture** -- partnering with French indie devs or getting listed in "Games Made in France" streams provides cultural credibility.
5. **JeuxVideo.com is the essential French gaming media** -- France's #1 gaming site with active community forums.
6. **Paris Games Week** provides massive mainstream exposure as Europe's second-largest gaming event (317K visitors).
