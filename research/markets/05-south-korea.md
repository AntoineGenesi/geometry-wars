# Market 5: South Korea

**Researched:** 2026-02-10
**Market Size:** 4th largest gaming market globally. Extremely competitive gaming culture. PC bang (gaming cafe) culture.
**Primary Platforms:** SOOP Korea (formerly AfreecaTV), Chzzk (Naver), YouTube, Naver Cafe, DCInside
**Language:** Korean

---

## Market Overview

South Korea has one of the world's most competitive gaming cultures, centered around esports (League of Legends, StarCraft, VALORANT). After Twitch shut down Korean operations in February 2024, the streaming landscape shifted to SOOP Korea (AfreecaTV rebrand) and Chzzk (Naver's platform, reached 100M hours watched by end of 2024). The indie game scene is growing, with G-Star in Busan featuring the largest-ever indie showcase (60 indie games in 2025). Korean VTubers are gaining popularity. The market is heavily esports-oriented, but variety streamers and indie game content are growing niches.

## Regional Platform Notes

- **SOOP Korea (formerly AfreecaTV)**: Dominant Korean streaming platform. Rebranded in 2024. Top categories: LoL (195M hours), Minecraft (139M), StarCraft (125M).
- **Chzzk (Naver)**: Naver's streaming platform launched after Twitch exit. Reached 100M hours watched by end of 2024. More similar to Western platforms in game variety.
- **PC Bang (PC Cafe)**: South Korea's unique gaming cafe culture. Players try games in PC Bangs before buying. Browser-based game could be played directly in PC Bangs.
- **Naver Cafe**: Community platform. Game-specific cafes are where dedicated fans gather.
- **DCInside**: Massive Korean image board/forum (2-3M daily visitors). Gaming galleries are very active.

## Key Takeaways for South Korea Market

1. **G-Star + BIC (Busan Indie Connect) are the primary event targets** -- G-Star's Indie Showcase 2.0 featured 60 indie games in 2025, and BIC is a dedicated indie game festival.
2. **The post-Twitch landscape creates opportunity** -- SOOP and Chzzk are both hungry for content variety beyond LoL/StarCraft. Indie games are an underserved niche.
3. **PC Bang culture is a unique advantage** -- browser-based game can be played directly in PC Bangs without installation, a significant distribution advantage.
4. **Korean VTubers are a growing channel** -- similar to Japan, VTubers play indie games and are gaining popularity rapidly on Chzzk.
5. **Inven and Ruliweb** are the primary gaming media targets for Korean-language coverage.
6. **Competitive gaming culture** means high-skill gameplay content resonates strongly -- showcasing the game's difficulty and skill ceiling would appeal to Korean audiences.
