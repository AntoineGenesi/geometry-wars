# Market 4: China

**Researched:** 2026-02-10
**Market Size:** World's largest gaming market (~$50B). Unique ecosystem with domestic platforms replacing Western services.
**Primary Platforms:** Bilibili, Douyin (TikTok CN), Huya, DouYu, TapTap, WeGame, Xiaohongshu (RedNote)
**Language:** Chinese (Simplified)

---

## Market Overview

China is the world's largest gaming market but operates in a completely separate ecosystem from the West. YouTube, Twitch, Discord, and Steam (partially) are replaced by domestic alternatives: Bilibili (video/streaming, 130M+ MAU), Huya (streaming, 120M+ MAU), DouYu (streaming, 160M+ MAU), Douyin (short-form video), and TapTap (200M+ users, mobile game community). The indie scene is growing rapidly, anchored by CiGA (China Indie Game Alliance) and events like WePlay Expo and IndiePlay Awards (650+ submissions in 2025). Browser-based games have additional regulatory considerations in China (game licenses), but the audience for indie/roguelite games is substantial and growing.

## Regional Platform Notes

- **Bilibili**: The primary platform for gaming content among young Chinese audiences. ACG-rooted culture aligns well with indie/bullet hell genres. 130M+ MAU.
- **Douyin**: China's TikTok. Short-form video can make games go viral overnight. 600M+ DAU.
- **Huya/DouYu**: Streaming platforms. Combined 280M+ MAU. Primarily esports but variety streams cover indie games.
- **TapTap**: Mobile game discovery platform. 200M+ users. Community reviews are the primary driver of game awareness.
- **WeGame**: Tencent's Steam competitor. Has indie game sections.
- **Xiaohongshu (RedNote)**: Social commerce platform turned gaming discovery tool. 300M+ MAU.
- **QQ**: Messaging platform (like Discord). Gaming groups are essential for community building.

---

## Regulatory Considerations

- Games need an ISBN license number to be officially published in China (process takes months)
- Browser-based games may have different regulatory requirements than downloadable games
- Working with a Chinese publisher/partner is typically necessary for market entry
- Bilibili, TapTap, and WeGame all require partnerships for game listing

## Key Takeaways for China Market

1. **WePlay Expo + IndiePlay Awards is the #1 event target** -- 650+ indie game submissions, 70+ games showcased, 20K+ visitors. CiGA runs both and is the gateway organization.
2. **Bilibili is the primary content platform** -- its ACG audience is perfectly aligned with bullet hell and roguelite genres. The word "danmu" (bullet curtain) is literally the same as "danmaku."
3. **TapTap is essential for discovery** -- 200M+ users, community-driven reviews. If the game is mobile-friendly (browser-based), TapTap is critical.
4. **Viral potential is enormous** -- Sultan's Game got 590M views on Xiaohongshu, Escape from Duckov sold 3M copies in a month. China's platforms can create overnight hits.
5. **Regulatory barriers are real** -- ISBN license required for official publishing. Working with a Chinese partner is typically necessary.
6. **The Touhou Chinese fanbase** is massive and directly relevant to our bullet hell genre.
