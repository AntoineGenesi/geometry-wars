# Market 9: Russia/CIS

**Researched:** 2026-02-10
**Market Size:** Large gaming market. Strong indie development tradition (Pathologic, Disco Elysium dev roots). CIS includes Ukraine, Belarus, Kazakhstan, etc.
**Primary Platforms:** VK Play Live, YouTube, Twitch (limited), Telegram, VKontakte
**Language:** Russian

---

## Market Overview

The Russian gaming market operates in a partially isolated ecosystem following Western platform restrictions. Twitch's influence has diminished, with VK Play Live (Tencent/VK partnership) rising as the primary Russian streaming platform. Telegram has overtaken VK in reach (78.3% vs 67.7%) and serves as both messaging and content platform. The indie dev scene has a storied history (Ice-Pick Lodge, Owlcat Games, Mundfish). DevGAMM is the largest CIS game developer conference. "Games from Russia" festival celebrates domestic indie development.

## Regional Platform Notes

- **VK Play Live**: Russian streaming platform integrated with VKontakte. Launched after Twitch's reduced presence. Growing indie game content.
- **Telegram**: #1 reach among Russian gamers (78.3%). Essential for game promotion. Gaming channels have massive subscriber counts.
- **VKontakte (VK)**: Russian social network (90M+ users). Gaming groups and communities.
- **YouTube**: Still accessible and popular in Russia for gaming content. Top Russian gaming channels have 10-19M+ subscribers.
- **Steam**: Remains accessible. Russian is one of Steam's top languages by user count.

---

## Key Takeaways for Russia/CIS Market

1. **Telegram is the #1 promotion channel** -- 78.3% reach among Russian gamers. Gaming channels there are essential for visibility.
2. **DevGAMM is the gateway event** -- largest CIS developer conference with 3,000+ attendees from 30+ countries.
3. **Kuplinov Play is the ideal creator target** -- 15M+ subscribers, plays indie and horror games. Energetic style suits action games.
4. **DTF.ru is the primary community platform** -- active indie game discussion and hosted "Games from Russia" festival content.
5. **CIS market includes significant audiences** beyond Russia: Ukraine, Belarus, Kazakhstan, etc. -- Russian-language content reaches all of them.
6. **Strong indie dev tradition** means the audience understands and appreciates indie games.
