# Market 20: Canada

**Researched:** 2026-02-10
**Market Size:** Strong gaming market. Major game development hub (Montreal, Vancouver, Toronto). Home of indie hits (Cuphead, Celeste). English/French bilingual.
**Primary Platforms:** YouTube, Twitch, Discord, Reddit
**Language:** English, French

---

## Market Overview

Canada is one of the world's most important game development hubs, with Montreal, Vancouver, and Toronto hosting major studios (Ubisoft Montreal, BioWare, and hundreds of indie studios). Canadian indie games have achieved massive global success (Cuphead, Celeste, Hades developer Supergiant was co-founded by a Canadian). The country's bilingual English/French audience overlaps significantly with both Market 1 (USA/English) and Market 8 (France). DreamHack Montreal brings international gaming culture to Canada. The Canadian indie dev community is thriving and well-connected.

## Key Takeaways for Canada Market

1. **Northernlion IS the Canadian gaming scene** for our genre -- based in Vancouver, he's the single most important outreach target globally for roguelite/indie content.
2. **Canadian indie dev community** is world-class (Cuphead, Celeste) and well-connected -- a recommendation from one studio cascades through the network.
3. **Montreal, Vancouver, Toronto** are major game dev hubs -- developer community events are ideal for organic outreach.
4. **DreamHack Montreal** provides LAN party culture where browser-based multiplayer games shine.
5. **Bit Bazaar in Toronto** is a perfect DIY/indie venue for community building.
6. **English/French bilingual** -- outreach covers both English and French markets simultaneously.
