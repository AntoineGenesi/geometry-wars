# Market 18: Mexico

**Researched:** 2026-02-10
**Market Size:** 68.7M gamers. Major Latin American gaming market. Growing esports scene (20.2% CAGR). 18M watched pro esports, 8.4M competed actively.
**Primary Platforms:** YouTube, Twitch, TikTok, Discord, Facebook
**Language:** Spanish

---

## Market Overview

Mexico has 68.7 million gamers and a rapidly growing esports scene (20.2% CAGR). The Electronic Game Show (EGS) in Mexico City has been running since 2002 and is the largest gaming convention in Mexico. GAMACON is Mexico's leading gaming industry conference, bringing together 20+ speakers from studios, publishers, and platforms. Mexican gamers overlap significantly with the Spanish-language market (Market 10) but have distinct local communities and events. Gaming is a major social connection tool in Mexican culture.
