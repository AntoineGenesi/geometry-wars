# Market 12: Australia / New Zealand

**Researched:** 2026-02-10
**Market Size:** Strong indie game development scene. PAX Aus is the largest gaming event in the Southern Hemisphere. English-speaking.
**Primary Platforms:** YouTube, Twitch, Discord, Reddit
**Language:** English

---

## Market Overview

Australia and New Zealand have a vibrant indie game development scene and host PAX Aus (Melbourne), the largest gaming event in the Southern Hemisphere. Melbourne International Games Week (MIGW) combines GCAP (developer conference), PAX Aus, and the Australian Game Developer Awards. The PAX Rising section showcases 100+ independent games from ANZ and worldwide. The Click Crew (LazarBeam, Loserfruit, Muselk) put Australian gaming content on the global map. Most ANZ gamers consume English content, overlapping with Markets 1 (USA) and 7 (UK).

## Key Takeaways for Australia/NZ Market

1. **PAX Aus + MIGW is the #1 event target** -- PAX Rising features 100+ indie games, and the Aus Indie Showcase highlights local developers.
2. **RhapsodyPlays is the ideal creator target** -- Australian, specializes in early-access roguelikes. Perfect genre fit.
3. **Melbourne is the ANZ indie game hub** -- GCAP, Freeplay, IGDA Melbourne, and multiple developer meetups.
4. **English-speaking market overlaps with USA/UK** -- creator outreach for Markets 1 and 7 partially covers ANZ audience.
5. **Time zone advantage** -- ANZ streamers are active during Asian prime time, reaching Japanese and Korean audiences too.
6. **Skill Up's analytical review style** would be ideal for a deep dive into our game's mechanics and design.
