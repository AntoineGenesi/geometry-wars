# Market 13: Poland

**Researched:** 2026-02-10
**Market Size:** Major gaming market in Central/Eastern Europe. Home of CD Projekt Red, 11 bit studios, Techland, Bloober Team. Strong gaming culture.
**Primary Platforms:** YouTube, Twitch, Steam, Discord
**Language:** Polish

---

## Market Overview

Poland has one of Europe's strongest gaming cultures, anchored by world-class studios like CD Projekt Red (The Witcher, Cyberpunk 2077), 11 bit studios (Frostpunk, This War of Mine), Techland (Dying Light), and Bloober Team (Layers of Fear). Poznan Game Arena (PGA) is the largest gaming event in Central/Eastern Europe with 60,000+ annual visitors. The indie scene benefits from Poland's strong game dev education and culture. Polish gamers are passionate, community-driven, and price-conscious -- a browser-based free-to-play game has strong appeal.

## Key Takeaways for Poland Market

1. **Poznan Game Arena is the #1 event target** -- largest gaming event in Central/Eastern Europe (60K+ visitors), with an impressive Indie Game Zone (50+ exhibitors, 80 games).
2. **Digital Dragons in Krakow** is the premier B2B game industry conference in Poland -- ideal for networking and business development.
3. **Polish game dev culture is world-class** -- CD Projekt Red, 11 bit studios heritage means Polish audiences understand and appreciate quality games.
4. **Price-conscious market** -- browser-based free-to-play games have strong appeal. Polish gamers actively seek great value.
5. **Pixel Heaven festival** specifically caters to indie and retro gaming fans -- perfect venue for a Geometry Wars-style arcade game.
6. **GRYOnline.pl** is the essential Polish gaming media outlet for coverage and community engagement.
