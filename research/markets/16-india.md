# Market 16: India

**Researched:** 2026-02-10
**Market Size:** Massive gaming population (500M+ gamers). Mobile-first market. 68.7M gamers in Mexico-equivalent data point for scale. Growing PC and browser gaming.
**Primary Platforms:** YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Discord
**Language:** Hindi, English, regional languages

---

## Market Overview

India has one of the world's largest gaming populations, but it is overwhelmingly mobile-first (BGMI/PUBG Mobile, Free Fire, Mobile Legends dominate). YouTube is the primary content consumption platform, with India being YouTube's largest market by users. Hindi-language gaming content has massive viewership. The PC/browser gaming audience is smaller but growing, concentrated in urban areas and among college-age demographics. India's top gaming YouTubers have 30-40M+ subscribers, rivaling global leaders. The indie game development scene is nascent but growing, with studios in Bangalore, Mumbai, and Delhi.

## Key Takeaways for India Market

1. **Mobile-first market** -- browser-based game accessible on mobile browsers could reach India's massive audience.
2. **r/IndianGaming is the primary community** target -- active subreddit with PC gaming enthusiasts who would appreciate a browser game.
3. **NASSCOM Game Developer Conference** is the industry event for developer networking.
4. **Hindi-language content** has massive viewership -- even a small Hindi-speaking creator partnership could reach millions.
5. **Price sensitivity is extreme** -- free browser-based game has enormous appeal in India's price-conscious market.
6. **College-age urban demographic** (PC/browser gamers) is the target segment within India's broader mobile-dominated market.
