**Researched:** 2026-02-10
**Market Size:** Strong indie game scene. Home of major gaming media (Eurogamer, Rock Paper Shotgun). Significant overlap with USA/English Global market.
**Primary Platforms:** YouTube, Twitch, Discord, Reddit, Twitter/X
**Language:** English

---

## Market Overview

The UK has one of the strongest indie game ecosystems in the world, with major gaming media outlets (Eurogamer, Rock Paper Shotgun, PC Gamer UK), influential events (EGX, its Rezzed indie zone), and a rich tradition of indie game development. The Yogscast network bridges content creation and indie game publishing. UK creators often have global English-speaking audiences, making outreach highly efficient. The UK also has a strong tradition of bedroom coders and indie developers, creating a community that appreciates and supports independent games.

1. **EGX + Rezzed zone is the #1 UK event target** -- the Rezzed zone was literally co-created by Rock Paper Shotgun for indie games. Perfect venue for our game.
2. **Rock Paper Shotgun is the #1 media target** -- they are known for championing indie games and literally co-created the UK's premier indie game showcase.
3. **PC Gamer already published an article about a "roguelike take on Geometry Wars" (Sektori)** -- our game fits their content perfectly.
4. **Angory Tom + Yogscast network** is a powerful channel -- Yogscast has a game publishing division and their network reaches millions of indie game fans.
5. **UK creators have global English-speaking audiences** -- outreach to UK creators effectively covers the entire English-speaking world.
6. **London Games Festival** provides city-wide visibility with mainstream media coverage (The Guardian, etc.).
