# Market 19: Middle East (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Egypt, etc.)

**Researched:** 2026-02-10
**Market Size:** Fastest-growing gaming region globally. Saudi Arabia is one of the fastest-growing countries in gaming/esports. Massive government investment (Saudi Arabia's Vision 2030 includes gaming).
**Primary Platforms:** YouTube, Twitch, TikTok, Discord, Instagram
**Language:** Arabic, English

---

## Market Overview

The Middle East is the world's fastest-growing gaming region, with Saudi Arabia and UAE leading in investment and adoption. Saudi Arabia's Vision 2030 includes massive gaming industry investment through Savvy Gaming Group ($38B+ committed). UAE and Saudi Arabia rank among the top 10 global markets for YouTube Gaming. The Arab Gamerz Awards celebrate gaming creators, developers, and esports in the Arab world. The audience skews young and male, with high engagement and spending power in Gulf states. Arabic gaming content is growing rapidly across YouTube and streaming platforms.

## Key Takeaways for Middle East Market

1. **Saudi Arabia's Vision 2030 gaming investment** ($38B+ through Savvy Gaming Group) makes this market strategically important -- rapid growth and government backing.
2. **YZNSA is the best creator target** -- known for thoughtful commentary, in-depth analysis, and playing indie games alongside mainstream titles.
3. **Gamers8 in Riyadh** is the premiere gaming event in the region with massive investment and attendance.
4. **Arab Gamerz Awards** provide a direct channel to the Arab gaming creator community.
5. **High spending power in Gulf states** -- even with a free game, Gulf audiences are willing to support premium features.
6. **YouTube Gaming is dominant** -- UAE and Saudi Arabia rank top 10 globally for YouTube Gaming engagement.
