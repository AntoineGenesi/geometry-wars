# Market 17: Southeast Asia (Philippines, Indonesia, Thailand, Vietnam, Malaysia, Singapore)

**Researched:** 2026-02-10
**Market Size:** $7.3B+ annual gaming revenue (2025). One of the fastest-growing gaming regions globally. Mobile-dominant but PC/browser growing.
**Primary Platforms:** YouTube, Facebook Gaming, TikTok, Twitch, Discord
**Language:** Filipino/Tagalog, Indonesian, Thai, Vietnamese, Malay, English

---

## Market Overview

Southeast Asia is one of the world's fastest-growing gaming markets, projected at $7.3B+ revenue by end of 2025. The region is mobile-dominant (Mobile Legends, Free Fire, PUBG Mobile), but PC gaming is growing especially in internet cafes (similar to Korean PC Bangs). Singapore leads in game development investment. Content creators have massive followings. Gamescom Asia x Thailand Game Show (launching 2026 in Bangkok) will bring a major international gaming event to the region. SEAGO (Southeast Asia Games Onward) is the annual digital showcase for SEA game development.

## Key Takeaways for SEA Market

1. **Internet cafe culture** is a unique advantage for browser-based games -- no installation needed, playable on cafe PCs.
2. **Gamescom Asia x Thailand Game Show (2026)** brings a major international event to SEA -- excellent opportunity for regional exposure.
3. **HEARTROCKER is the best creator target** -- Thailand's "Horror Game King" with 9.4M subscribers who plays variety including indie games.
4. **SEAGO showcase** is the primary channel for reaching SEA's indie-aware audience.
5. **Mobile-first market** means browser accessibility on phones is critical.
6. **Massive population** (700M+ across SEA) with rapidly growing gaming adoption -- early presence in this market has long-term value.
