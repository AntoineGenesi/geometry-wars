# Market 10: Spain / Latin America

**Researched:** 2026-02-10
**Market Size:** Spanish-speaking world: 500M+ speakers. Spain + 19 Latin American countries. Growing indie scene especially in Spain, Argentina, Mexico.
**Primary Platforms:** YouTube, Twitch, TikTok, Discord, Twitter/X
**Language:** Spanish

---

## Market Overview

The Spanish-speaking gaming market is enormous and culturally unified by language, though with regional differences. Spain has a rapidly growing indie game development scene (Barcelona and Madrid are hubs), while Latin American audiences are massive streaming consumers (Ibai Llanos, ElRubiusOMG). Spanish-language creators often have audiences spanning both Spain and Latin America. Barcelona hosts IndieDevDay/BCN Game Fest, and the GameLab congress draws 1,000+ professionals annually. The gaming industry in Spain has "exploded" with the indie boom, reaching international quality levels.

## Key Takeaways for Spain/Latin America Market

1. **Barcelona is the indie game capital of Spain** -- BCN Game Fest, GameLab (1,000+ professionals), GameBCN accelerator, Barcelona Games World (135K+ attendees).
2. **Spanish-language content reaches 500M+ speakers** -- a single Spanish creator can reach audiences across Spain and 19 Latin American countries.
3. **IlloJuan and retro game enthusiasts** are the best creator targets -- IlloJuan specifically plays retro games, and arcade-style games resonate with his audience.
4. **DayoScript provides high-quality analysis** -- in-depth video essays that would give our game thoughtful, long-form coverage.
5. **3DJuegos is the primary Spanish gaming media** target with active community forums.
6. **Games from Spain** organization promotes Spanish-made games internationally -- potential partnership for localization and cross-promotion.
