# Market 6: Brazil

**Researched:** 2026-02-10
**Market Size:** Largest gaming market in Latin America. Massive streaming culture. Portuguese language.
**Primary Platforms:** Twitch, YouTube, Kick, Discord, Twitter/X
**Language:** Portuguese (Brazilian)

---

## Market Overview

Brazil is the largest gaming market in Latin America with one of the world's most passionate streaming communities. Brazilian streamers consistently rank among the most-watched globally on Twitch. The country hosts Gamescom LATAM + BIG Festival in Sao Paulo (the world's largest indie games competition, 962 entries from 71 countries in 2025). Brazilian gaming culture is highly social and community-driven, with strong engagement on Twitch, YouTube, and Discord. The indie scene is growing rapidly, with 19 Brazilian titles among the 90 BIG Festival finalists in 2025.

## Key Takeaways for Brazil Market

1. **Gamescom LATAM + BIG Festival is the #1 event target** -- world's largest indie games competition (962 entries). Being a finalist provides massive visibility across all of Latin America.
2. **Cellbit is the #1 creator outreach target** -- he's both a content creator and indie game developer. His support of indie games is authentic and his audience expects it.
3. **Brazilian streaming culture is incredibly social** -- Twitch, YouTube, and Kick all have massive Brazilian audiences. Games that create entertaining moments for streamers spread quickly.
4. **The Brazilian indie dev scene is growing fast** -- 19 Brazilian titles among 90 BIG Festival finalists shows strong local indie culture.
5. **Portuguese localization would significantly increase reach** -- Brazil + Portugal represents a 250M+ Portuguese-speaking audience.
6. **Brasil Game Show (BGS)** with 300K+ visitors is the largest gaming event in LATAM and provides mainstream exposure.
